Our Story

Our Ganesh Chaturthi Celebration

Every year, the people of Rajannagudem come together to welcome Lord Ganesha with devotion, happiness, and joy. For ten days, families, friends, children, and elders celebrate together with daily aarti, bhajans, cultural programs, prasadam, and many happy moments. Ganesh Chaturthi is more than just a festival for us. It brings our community together and reminds us of the importance of faith, unity, and togetherness. From morning prayers to evening celebrations, every moment is special when we celebrate together.

🪔

Daily Aarti

Morning and evening prayers offered together, every single day of the celebration.

🙏

Community Puja

Rituals led by our priests, open to every family who wishes to seek Bappa's blessings.

🎉

Cultural Programs

Music, dance, and performances from our children, youth, and elders alike.

🍲

Prasadam & Annadanam

Shared meals and offerings, because no celebration is complete until everyone has eaten.
